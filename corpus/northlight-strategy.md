# Northlight Records: strategy 2027 to 2029

## Who we are

Northlight Records is an independent music company based in London. It has three parts:

- **The label** signs artists and releases their recordings. It holds a catalogue of about 6,000 releases and works with 40 active artists.
- **Northlight Publishing** manages the songs of about 300 songwriters. It registers their works, collects their royalties and licenses their songs.
- **The Northlight shop** sells vinyl, CDs, T-shirts and tour items directly to fans online.

About 140 people work at Northlight. Around 70 of them work in operations, across seven teams: royalties and rights, publishing admin, sync licensing, digital distribution and metadata, physical supply chain, customer services and artist services.

## Where we are today

Our artists and writers stay with us because we treat them fairly. Our weak points are in the back office. Royalty statements take too long and contain too many errors. Release metadata is often wrong or incomplete when it reaches streaming services. Much of the work runs on spreadsheets, shared inboxes and the knowledge of a few experienced people.

## Our vision

By 2029, every artist, writer and fan who deals with Northlight gets it right first time: correct money, correct credits, quick answers.

## Our aims

### Aim 1. Pay artists and writers correctly and on time

- Cut errors in royalty statements by 30% by the end of 2027.
- Issue statements within 30 days of the end of each period, down from 60 days.
- Reduce unmatched publishing income, money we collect but cannot yet assign to a song or writer, by 25% by 2028.

### Aim 2. Get every release right everywhere

- Keep metadata errors at delivery below 1% of releases by the end of 2027.
- Deliver every new release to all streaming services at least four weeks before release day.
- Complete songwriter and performer credits for 95% of the catalogue by 2029.

### Aim 3. Grow income from our catalogue

- Grow sync income by 20% by 2028.
- Answer sync licence requests within two working days, down from five.
- Grow streams of releases older than five years by 10% a year.

### Aim 4. Serve fans and partners well

- Answer 90% of customer and retailer queries within one working day.
- Process shop refunds and returns within five working days.
- Cut damaged stock returned by retailers by 20% by 2028.

### Aim 5. Work well with AI, safely

- Every operations team has a trained AI champion by mid 2027.
- No AI output reaches an artist, writer, fan or partner without a person checking it.
- Raise the share of staff who say their workload is manageable by 10 points in the annual survey by 2028.

## How we will work

- We judge AI ideas by the quality of service they give artists, writers, fans and partners, not by the time they save.
- We test new ideas with a small pilot and a measured baseline before we scale them.
- We protect artists' and fans' personal data, and we follow UK GDPR.
- We measure success by outcomes for artists, writers and fans, not by activity.
