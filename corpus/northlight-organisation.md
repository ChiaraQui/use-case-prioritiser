# Northlight Records: operations teams

About 70 of Northlight's 140 people work in operations, in seven teams. The head of operations leads them, and the AI owner sits in operations.

Everyone uses Microsoft 365: Outlook, Excel, Word, SharePoint and Teams. The approved AI tools are Microsoft 365 Copilot, Claude and ChatGPT.

| Team | People | Main aims served |
|---|---|---|
| Royalties and rights | 14 | Aim 1 |
| Publishing admin | 12 | Aim 1, Aim 2 |

## Royalties and rights (14 people)

Calculates what the label owes its artists, produces their statements and keeps the rights database accurate: who owns which recording, in which territories, on what terms.

- **Main work:** importing sales and streaming reports from distributors and services, matching them to recordings and contracts, running statements every quarter, answering artists' questions about their statements.
- **Tools:** Curve for royalty accounting, a rights database in FileMaker, Excel for exceptions, an Outlook shared mailbox. Artists see their statements in the Curve portal.
- **Where work gets stuck:** income lines that do not match a recording, contract terms held in PDFs rather than in the system, statement queries that need someone to dig back through several reports.

## Publishing admin (12 people)

Manages the songs of about 300 songwriters: registers new works, records who wrote what share, collects publishing royalties and pays the writers.

- **Main work:** registering songs with collecting societies such as PRS and MCPS, checking writer splits, matching incoming publishing income to songs, chasing missing registrations, paying writers.
- **Tools:** Curve for publishing royalties, a works database in FileMaker, the PRS for Music portal, Excel, an Outlook shared mailbox. Writers see their statements in the Curve portal.
- **Where work gets stuck:** income that cannot be matched to a song (unmatched income), disagreements on writer splits, songs registered late or with wrong details.

## Sync licensing (5 people)

Licenses recordings and songs for film, TV, ads and games.

- **Main work:** answering licence requests, checking who owns the recording and the song, getting approval from artists and writers, quoting fees, drafting and sending licences.
- **Tools:** Synchtank for requests and deals, DISCO for sharing tracks with clients, the rights and works databases in FileMaker, licence templates in Word.
- **Where work gets stuck:** checking ownership across two databases, waiting for approvals, drafting licences from templates by hand. Requests take five working days on average.

## Digital distribution and metadata (10 people)

Delivers every release to Spotify, Apple Music and the other services, with the correct codes, credits and artwork.

- **Main work:** preparing release metadata (titles, codes, credits, release dates), delivering through FUGA, fixing errors the services reject, updating the catalogue.
- **Tools:** FUGA for delivery to the streaming services and for the catalogue, release plans in Excel on SharePoint.
- **Where work gets stuck:** credits arriving late or incomplete from artist teams, errors found only after a service rejects a delivery, old catalogue with missing songwriter credits.

## Physical supply chain (8 people)

Manages vinyl and CD manufacturing, stock and returns.

- **Main work:** placing pressing orders, tracking stock at the warehouse, handling retailer orders and returns, recording damaged stock.
- **Tools:** Linnworks for stock and orders, the fulfilment partner's warehouse system, supplier email in Outlook, Excel for returns.
- **Where work gets stuck:** returns reported in different formats by each retailer, damaged stock claims checked by hand, stock figures that do not match between the warehouse and the spreadsheets.

## Customer services (13 people)

Answers fans who buy from the Northlight shop and retailers who stock Northlight releases.

- **Main work:** answering order, delivery and refund questions, processing refunds and returns, handling retailer queries about orders and invoices.
- **Tools:** Shopify for the shop, Zendesk for the help desk, an Outlook shared mailbox, Linnworks for order status.
- **Where work gets stuck:** peaks around big releases and pre-orders, the same questions answered by hand many times, refunds that need information from the warehouse.

## Artist services (8 people)

Looks after the label's own artists from signing to release.

- **Main work:** onboarding new artists, planning release schedules, collecting credits and artwork, coordinating with marketing, answering artists' questions, including questions about their statements.
- **Tools:** release plans in Excel on SharePoint, Outlook, Teams, SharePoint folders for artwork and credits.
- **Where work gets stuck:** collecting complete credits from artists and producers, answering statement questions that need the royalties team, keeping release plans up to date across teams.

## Rules for AI ideas

The AI owner applies these rules when assessing an idea.

1. Legal approval is needed for AI on signed contracts and licences. Deal memos are internal notes of agreed terms and need no approval.
2. A narrower idea inside a broader one is a separate idea, linked to the broader one.
3. Who submits an idea never changes its score.
4. A risk 0 idea worth doing goes to Next, with a note to redesign it with a person approving.
5. Cite Aim 5 only when the idea's main purpose is AI skills, safe AI use, or staff workload and wellbeing. An idea which helps staff indirectly scores fit 1 at most under Aim 5.
